Sequence

    Every layer has its own clock

    1–3 s per decisionNeural network (server)

    Reads the whole scene and writes the tool call token by token. The slowest layer and the only one that reasons.

    ≈ 50–200 msInternet

    Encrypted round trip between the server and the workshop. Short drops happen; the design must tolerate them.

    < 5 msMCP server (local PC)

    Turns move_object(…) into a concrete task and returns the result: ok or grip failed.

    ≈ 30 HzLearned policy (VLA)

    Local model such as SmolVLA or ACT. Watches the cameras and outputs chunks of joint actions.

    ≈ 1 kHzFirmware

    PID loop per joint, current limits and emergency stop. Always running, also to hold the arm still.

    PWM 50–330 HzServos and encoders

    Move the joint and report its real position back to the firmware and the policy.

    The layers, nodes and connections of the neural network are a schematic: a real model has billions of parameters and its internal architecture is not public. Timings are typical orders of magnitude, not measurements of a real system.
